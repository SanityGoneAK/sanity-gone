# Should You Pull Violet Mizutsune Orchid?

Most likely, yes. Violet Mizutsune Orchid is a collab limited, one of the best Snipers in the game, and comes at a low pull cost compared to other banners. It makes for a situation few people will want to skip!

...however. There's always a however.

Compared to the pure meta insanity lately, most of which is still coming up, Orchid2 is one of the weaker units. That isn't to say she is not great, or meta, because she is both! Just weak*er*. Things are so insane of late that Orchid2 can feel a bit weaker than the likes of Kal2, Angie2, or Makoto, who are some of the most insane units to ever grace the game. Plus, with regards to my above statement of being "one of the best Snipers in the game", that's partially a reflection of Snipers currently being one of the weakest archetypes. Crazy to say that with Wiš'adel as a member (remember Wiš'adel?), but the top crust of Snipers do tend to be a bit less insane than the top crust of the other archetypes right now.

So if you burned all your pulls on Kal2, and are one of the rare few who don't care about limited FOMO, you'll probably survive without Orchid2.

As for her kit itself, it's something I'm quite shocked we haven't gotten again until now. April, my favorite unit if you didn't know, came out a long time ago now, and I remember quite distinctly how everyone, including me, naturally assumed we'd get a 6★ version of her in short order. That never materialized until now, six years later, with only a tiny handful of units even coming kinda close¹, which is a bit odd considering how useful April has proven to be! Reduced redeployment, coupled with survivability, is a great combo. April held a lot of value over time, despite her rarity (she has arguably aged better than many 6★s have), because this is a very unique thing. Orchid2, as a 6★, of course ramps that up even further allowing for faster damage, multiple-targets, more flexible deployments, and a longer range!

This isn't just an excuse to talk about my wife, although I'll admit that did make it easier to include, but hopefully to rather illustrate why Orchid2 is valuable to own even if she isn't "as powerful" as some other options. Her value is relatively unique, and that flexibility can oftentimes solve problems that raw power simply can't. Although all these crazy 6★s lately also have completely unique utility too...

It's not all upside though, and there's a few notable downsides. First, her DPH on her main skill (S2) is on the low side for a modern unit. While it's certainly not a bad value, it's low enough to be noticeable on harder targets. In theory, that role was probably meant to be filled by her S3 instead, but that skill generally ends up being more trouble than it’s worth between the single-line range and long wind-up. Additionally, she's not as invulnerable as April is. While Take-Off is tremendously powerful, there is a delay before she actually gets into the air, as well as a gap between the first and second skill activations, so she sometimes can't even be placed as aggressively as you'd like.

Back on the upside though is the value of collab banners, which are about as valuable as banners get. First, they're solo rate-ups, which is the best kind for a given unit (although limited banners have better total odds if both units are good). More important though, the pity is hard at 120 pulls, reduced to just 100 after accounting for the free pulls. Considering standard banners have a soft pity that doesn't kick in until 150 (meaning 220ish is the hard pity) and limited banners are set at 300, the low hard pity on these banners is tremendous value!

To close this out, what this basically comes down to is just how tough this stretch of banners is. *Everyone* is great (even Aphrissa). It makes it really hard to tell you to skip any of them, but it also means it's hard to tell you to roll for everyone too, unless you get lucky. It's the nature of the gacha, I suppose, and we've been lucky in previous years to have had relatively skippable stretches with lesser limited pressure. That's no longer the case as HG seems to be pushing this power streak hard. So it'll ultimately be up to you how to value things. I really like Orchid2 and I don't think I could suggest skipping a collab unit that might not get rerun, buuuuuuuut if you can look past that, this is probably the second most skippable banner after Aphrissa, especially with Angelina the Mellow Wish only a couple months away.

¹ Incidentally, they’re also all ARC-X Heavyshooters, although Pozy also has her summon. In any case, none of them really ever came that close to the same use case.

# Should You Pull Kirin R Yato (rerun)?

I rarely write much about rerun banners, but Yato2 is an exceptional case (I'll probably do the same for Tragodia's rerun during Aphrissa's write-up), so I'll yap about Yato2 a little as well. 

Yes, you should roll on Yato2, at least if you can afford it. When she was new there was a lot of back and forth between Yato2 and Texas2, but time has clearly given the crown to Yato2 and she retains her value to this day despite the considerable powercreep. For whatever reason, HG pumped the brakes on FRDs of almost any kind. With the exception of Crownslayer and the Agents, and now Orchid2, there really haven't been many since (yes there are a few more but they’re inconsequential), and it's a very useful class to own! Yato2's range, instant application, high DPS, and hit-count/AoE utility have given her pretty consistent value since. Now, granted the higher power ceiling has meant utility like this is generally less necessary, and FRDs have rarely been good in CC style content (with the exception of Agents), but the point is more that Yato2 is still an overall valuable unit despite her age, and this is a rare chance to pick up an old collab unit.

Now if you can only afford one, I would favor Orchid2. When Orchid2 first came out, I was higher on the Yato2 rerun than Orchid2, but that's changed since. There's a few reasons for this. Most notably, I'm just higher on Orchid2 than I was initially. You would think I of all people would appreciate the value of an FRD Sniper! But there are a few other reasons too. One is related, which is that Yato2 is much more likely to be powercrept, and kinda already is as the power ceiling ever rises. It's been a while since we've had a new FRD, and even longer since we've had a non-welfare one. But my beautiful wife, who is still great and valid, shows how rare these sorts of Snipers are. She was literally the only one for 6 years! It feels like the odds of a direct powercreep to Orchid2 are pretty minimal. Orchid2's banner is also more valuable thanks to the free pulls. As a rerun collab banner, Yato2 doesn't get any. At a 120 maximum, Yato2's is still quite valuable, but it is more than the 100 for Orchid2. And last, there's the opportunity cost argument. Orchid2 is first, so favoring Yato2 would mean missing your odds at both entirely.

Still though, these are mostly nitpicks. Yato2 is still the best in her archetype, which is one of the best. She is still an important utility. She is still a collab that *probably* won't ever get another rerun. And her banner is still valuable. If you can afford it, you should still absolutely pull for her. This discussion is more about what happens if you feel like you can only afford one without compromising your chances at Angelina the Mellow Wish in a couple months.

# FAQ

## **Q:** Where does Violet Mizutsune Orchid fit into the meta-Sniper landscape?

**A:** Like April before her, Orchid2 is a bit of a curve ball in terms of relative meta-evaluation. Units with reduced redeployment are so *different* compared to more typical units, that the metrics against which they're judged have to be measured at a different scale. This is true with more classical FRDs like the Executors as well, but with the Snipers, they're so completely different that you can't really make a good comparison to *anyone*.

This was exactly the case with April, and why she's stayed relatively high on the tier list for her rarity while the other Marksman, including the original 6★s, have fallen quite a bit. Marksman were, and still are, the most populous group, but it was hard to say if April was better or worse than Platinum or Blue Poison because she was just so different!

And that's basically the case again with Orchid2. Personally, and this isn't just my bias talking, I tend to favor uniqueness when it comes to making a ranking if the standard comparisons can't be done 1:1. So I tend to be relatively high on Orchid2 compared to many others. I already have half-a-dozen top end Snipers (or Sniper-likes) that more or less can be boiled down to "do big pp damage"!

So in my opinion, Orchid2 is my number 2 Sniper. I have a tough time putting her ahead of Wiš'adel still, but I think her flexibility does put her ahead of Ray, Lemuen, and Narantuya. In fact, I wouldn't be surprised if Orchid2 pulls ahead of Wiš'adel at sometime in the future, because Wiš'adel can theoretically be powercrept, and given how things are going lately, that seems likely and not just possible, while Orchid2 will likely retain that unique edge!

However, to close out this thought, I do want to emphasize that this does tend to be a playstyle consideration as well. Orchid2 does have flaws, and some fairly major ones (low-ish DPH, a gap in her evasion, and very clunky alternative skills). The FRD playstyle doesn't click well with everyone either, particularly for those who don't enjoy a more active playstyle (no judgement, you do you). There will be people out there who find her to easily be the worst of the top 6★s! But me? I personally love her!

Ultimately, while DP is an expendable resource that *could* justify some effects, it still isn't free, and Catapult's implementation of it is just far too weak.

Most notably, this meant the new IS iterations tended to get moved until after the anniversary, when they typically come before the summer banner on CN, which was intensely irritating to many. That out of the way, I fear this skipping might happen again with IS#7. I would love to be wrong here, but if you thought there was a lot of dead time in the weeks leading up to Episode 17, well, that's because there was. Compared to CN, we lost nearly two full weeks of time between now and when the Necrass rerun happened. According to Y_Jun's datamines, which are very reliable, CCBP#5 begins on December 10th. That means it ends on December 24th. That's *exactly* three weeks until the anniversary date, which is the minimum time required for IS (one week for the leading Vignette, two weeks for the launch itself). There is still time here, but at the very least it means the Act or Die rerun will get moved back. There definitely isn't enough time for both without overlap, which they rarely do.

Now, it really is too early to tell for sure. Minor overlaps are possible, and there is still technically enough time. There's nothing special about the two weeks after an iteration launch either, so the full three isn't technically required. I absolutely hope I'm wrong here because I would love to have a new iteration to play over Christmas break instead of waiting two extra months for it! But at the very least, either IS#7 or Act or Die is likely going to get moved.

The worst part is none of this needed to happen. All they had to do was run the events in order and on time. Fuck Yostar.

Amid the sea of hyper-meta units that have been the norm of late, Violet Mizutsune Orchid is a somewhat more novel unit. The non-Specialist FRD space is an underutilized one that HG rarely treads on (April was SIX years ago!). That makes Orchid2 one of the more unique units in recent memory, and the flexibility she brings, both in terms of where she can be deployed and how fast, makes her a great tool to have on your roster. This comes at a small cost to her DPS, which tends not to be as crazy as other recent units, however the flexibility she brings allows that DPS to be better placed exactly where it's needed, meaning she's still an exceptionally powerful unit, albeit one that requires a bit more effort.

Before diving into her Masteries, you should actually first start with her base Module. Although some of it comes from her Talent, which separates her from the other ARC-X users, the 25 seconds of redeployment time her mod1 provides is vitally important to her flexibility. However, her upgrades, although still powerful and you should plan to get them, are less vital, so can be saved for after primary Mastery, which is itself important.

With her base ARC-X in hand, her primary Mastery, and only for many, will be her S2 which plays best with her reduced redeployment. With it, she Takes-Off, where she becomes immune to most damage and targeting, leaving her safe to rain down a hail of arrows over a wide area. Don't be fooled by the reference to firing X number of arrows which to many would imply a single-target. It's actually a True-AoE (don't ask me how that works) with the number of arrows representing the instances of damage. Importantly, she gets a free activation that triggers immediately, after which her second charge is usually ready, allowing for a very fast burst of damage.

Mastery is important due to the initial SP cost improvements, which, despite what may appear to be a small gain of only 3 SP, make a large difference since at S2M3 she can functionally activate twice in a row, while S2SL7 has a frustratingly long gap that leaves her very vulnerable. That's vitally important for a hit-and-run (or rather hit-from-safety) unit like Orchid2. The behavior here is actually fairly unusual. Despite being charge-based, she still suffers from SP-lockout, meaning she doesn't gain additional SP during that free activation. However, there is a short delay before it where she does generate SP. At SL7 this leaves her waiting around 4 seconds for her second activation, but it's only around 1 at M3. Since she is reliant on the Take-Off portion for safety, that ends up being a massive gap. That alone is worth a high priority Mastery consideration, but it's worth noting that the damage gain itself is fairly average, although on a relatively low DPH skill like this, every bit of ATK does matter.

After that, you should likely consider her ARC-X Module level 3 before any secondary skills, as the mod3 upgrade is important and both alternative skills are somewhat flawed. If you enjoy the playstyle enough to consider one though, it gets a bit complicated, but the fundamental suggestion is to probably not strongly fuss about it unless you have a specific need or a lot of resources. Most people looking for a second should consider her S3 first. However, the 3 second draw-time on it is very awkward, it takes unreasonably long for her second shot to be ready, and straight-line ranges are rarely good. In fact, it's the fatal flaw of the very worst 6★ Sniper, Fartooth, although Orchid2 at least has a lot of flexibility in her deployment which mitigates it somewhat. The damage isn't even particularly compelling for the awkwardness either, except against the large multi-tile bosses, which are a relative minority. However, it does have uses now and then, and the Masteries are fairly strong. In addition to the typical damage increase, there's a breakpoint at S3M1 that allows her to deploy with a charge ready, and another at S3M3 which increases the Force, although that part is easy to forget is even there. Still, creative players and those who want a bit more out of Orchid2 will find it to have some value.

Of that latter group, some may actually prefer her S1, although many units these days do it far better. It's basically her AFK skill, and can output some pretty reasonable and consistent damage, entirely hands off. However, that's a pretty pointless thing on a Fast-Redeploy unit. It's simply not what Orchid2 is good at or used for, and even if you are an extreme AFK player, you'll be better off with almost anyone else since virtually every new 6★ these days has an AFK or low effort skill of some kind. She doesn't even get an expanded range on it! However, it does have fairly strong gains, and even has an unusual M2 breakpoint which grants an extra charge, although it isn't labeled here due to the low value of the skill. There are a few people out there who will find value in the skill, but most, even the AFK players, will find better value for their resources elsewhere.

While Violet Mizutsune Orchid explores a relatively unused niche, her 5★ counterpart is a far weaker showing, illustrating little of the potential behind the Mercenary archetype. DP is an underutilized resource, but it's not free either, and Zinogre S Catapult simply doesn't do enough to justify that drain. She provides only damage, and fairly weak damage at that, and there are many 5★ Guards out there who will do more without the drawback, ultimately meaning few should consider raising her at all. However, if you're one of the few who isn't traumatized by her base version’s 3★ recruit line (it didn't use to be as fast, and took forever to get through on every recruit), she does enough to at least be functional.

If that's you, there's really not a standout skill, and you should probably plan to do both, or pick based on your planned use. Her S2 is the more unique one, with the greater potential to do something unique. Meanwhile, her S1 will probably be the better "daily-driver" type, but it's much less interesting too, with a much lower ceiling since it only offers some fairly minor hit-count utility, which the rarity already has in abundance. For that reason, the grades here give a slight favor to her S2.

Starting with that, the key feature worth noting is that it can be spammed. That's a use that's easy to miss since a lot of her kit revolves around powering up with additional charges (which S2 does as well), but the best use here is actually that you can activate it after only 2 SP, then immediately do the equipment activation for the big burst. Supposing you can spare 15 DP each time at least. That restriction is a daunting one, since skill spam by its nature will drain your DP very fast. Still, there are a handful of places it can be used to great effect, and the rarity doesn't have many units who can do anything similar. For the strength of gains themselves, they're slightly above average. It's a decent DPS gain, but not a crazy one either.

Her S1 meanwhile is only really useful with the extended duration her charges give. The 20 DP cost for a single activation is staggering, and the default duration of only 4 seconds is not worth the effect. However, with a full (or near full) complement of charges, it can output some decent DPS against multiple targets, with a more reasonable, although still relatively short, duration. If you like Catapult enough to regularly use her, this will make for a passable everyday skill, and the transition to Arts damage even resolves DPH issues. However, the cost is still staggering and the DPS is overall nothing special. Again, the gains here are slightly above average but not crazy. They're worthwhile if you actually intend to use her.

The stall portion often requires even more micromanagement, so a little caution is warranted.

Violet Mizutsune Orchid - Orchid2 is a Fast-Redeploy Sniper that ends up being quite flexible. She can be deployed to melee tiles, and has a pretty big range and multiple-targets. Considering that Snipers have tended to struggle in Advanced content, this flexibility has actually given Orchid2 a leg up, especially in IS#7. In some strange (i.e. Advanced) ways, she could actually be the best Sniper! However, that says more about the state of Snipers than it does about Orchid2. Although she’s great, her DPS isn’t as crazy and we’ve gotten used to, and many other upcoming units will be more valuable. Despite that, as a collab unit, you probably don’t want to miss out on her.

Kirin R Yato - In years past, Yato2 has shown the problem with meta units being in collabs. She is by far the best FRD in the game, but was forever locked to new players. FRDs tend to retain some of their value (relatively) even as the power increases and Yato2 is still a top-tier unit. Her DPS has waned a bit, but her utility more than makes up for it still to this day. If you don’t already have her, you probably don’t want to miss your chance to pick her up!

Clementia - Clem is almost my ideal DPS Guard. She has great DPS, she has range, she has a way of working around DEF/RES, and she has some unique and amazing utility that's fun to use. Personally, I love her! However, she's also a non-limited unit coming after a long string of limited units and just before yet another one. Guards are also the most crowded class by far. So while I think she could be one the best Guards right now (toss up with her, Chen3, and Ulpi), she's still probably going to end up being a skip for most f2p players, although she can absolutely justify the pulls if you don't care for limited FOMO.

Thumpy - Thumpy brings a strong combination of bulk and quality damage, which makes her a pretty decent unit overall. That’s not an especially unique combination, nor is she overwhelmingly good at either, but she also has some very unique shift-esque mechanics that make her both fun and novel, and creative oriented players may find her the most appealing upcoming unit. So she’ll be valuable to some, but in the meta-sense, amid a sea of hyper powerful limiteds, she’ll be a skip for many if you get Angie2 first.

Aphrissa - Aphrissa is a decent unit and a creative twist on the Blast Caster archetype. I personally love her kit design! However, the archetype is also innately underpowered. Her S3 has the numbers behind it to keep up, but is still pretty far behind the top Casters, and her other two skills don’t have the numbers, which kills a lot of potential flexibility.

# Lookaheads

New this update is the Yet Another Wave patch, and one I quite like. What's this? A fun Guard that synergizes with my new bunny? An actually good 5★? A fun new character??? The fish storyline isn't one of my favorites, but damn if I won't make an exception here.

## Clementia

I've run out of ways to describe ultra powerful characters, and Clementia is yet another one. She's not quite *as* insane as some of the other recent ones, but that's a very high bar, and she's still ridiculous herself. Fundamentally, she's a DPS Guard, and does about everything you'd want a DPS Guard to do. Her damage is great. She has ways to work around DEF/RES walls. She has range. She even has some unique utility for the creative-play crowd. All-in-all, she's just a solid general DPS unit with some good ceiling too. She's just not quite as broken as the EX-tier we're getting used to.

The nature of being a Primal Guard here is worth a quick discussion, even in this lookahead. In the past, I've said a lot about how the reliance of the Primal Casters/Guards on an applicator kills their value. But HG seems to have finally figured out the formula for them, first with Mantra and now with Clementia. They now seem to have their own unique value that plays into their Element, and instead the applicator just opens up some new doors. So Clementia does not need Thumpy to function at all, but they still work incredibly well together.

As for her skills, she's a very strong M6 candidate, and both S2 and S3 will be well graded with different uses, and most people who plan to pull for her and raise her will definitely want both. However, S2 will end up with the higher grades (for both Story and Advanced), although I do find the gap between them to be closer than many do, especially if you have Thumpy. S2 has her unique Shift utility¹, the details of which will have to wait for the full article, but like Thumpy, has the potential to be gamebreaking in places, and which makes it the more unique skill. It also cycles very fast and will have the better DPS for people who don't mind a little button pressing, as well as better handling DEF-walls if you don't have Thumpy. Meanwhile, her S3 performs double duty as both a high-quality AFK skill since she holds her ammo (no ammo is spent if the Corrosion burst kills the enemy), and as a massive true-ish damage nuke. However, it does have a tougher time against those DEF-walls if she isn't paired with Thumpy as her own application is neutered by the DEF. In terms of priority, it's just slightly lower ceiling than her S2, and more dependent on Thumpy, who many people will likely skip if Angie2 comes home first. But I do love the dual value behind it, as well as the global AoE True-ish damage potential.

¹ This is the second recent instance we have of non-traditional Shift skill, and curiously, both are on Corrosion units (the other is Thumpy's S3). That probably doesn't mean anything, but it is interesting to note.

tl;dr S2 and S3 both S-tier grades. S2 will be the higher of the two, and S3 may even slip to A-tier.

## de Toledo

De Toledo is better than the recent average for the rarity, but that isn't a particularly high compliment. She's a unit that I find myself thinking "she's fine, I guess." Not exactly bad, with a couple unique things going for her, but nothing particularly great either, and isn't even better than the classic 5★s Hexers. If you're willing to put in the resources, her total Fragile is actually a solid 42%, which is high, even outside the rarity. However, in niche clears, the blocking requirement is a tough one, and in non-niche clears, it isn't necessary in the first place. Meanwhile, her free blocker has some solid stats and a good Fear uptime, but is ultimately doomed by her 50 second cycle time. It's usable, and Beeswax's version of it has had some niche use over the years, but isn't particularly special either. Pass on her S1, which is much much worse than Shamare's S2 and is saddled with a massive SP cost.

tl;dr S2 C-tier or ungraded. Pass on S1.

## Homebound

Homebound is a pleasant surprise, and is the first good 5★ since Cairn (or Ukusik if you're an IS sweat, but I digress). He has some great utility, and really solid total AoE damage thanks to his long lasting Alchemical Unit. Of course, by meta-standards, his damage is still low, but for 5★ niche standards, his total damage of ~36k-ish Arts is superb. Then he gets a strong snare effect. And it only has a 10 second downtime! Then he gets a conditionless hit-rate debuff too! Which all makes him a very solid niche unit that should have some decent viability for those who favor the lower rarities. The downside is that it only works on heavier units (well, weight 3 minimum, which isn't actually heavy), and there are times where that will matter (although you can use his S1 instead if you really want), but generally that's a perfectly fine trade off. Plus he has the only, as of his release, base skill for the new facility and doesn't even need E2 to access it! He's a very pleasant breath of fresh air at a rapidly dying rarity!

The skill that does all that is his S2 which will be his primary skill and should grab a pretty decent grade, especially since it has reasonably strong gains. His S1 meanwhile may have some use for the husbando players. Interestingly, it's nearly the same skill, but with lower numbers and without the weight requirement. That means it'll be useful at times he needs to deal with 0-2 weight swarms, but for most players, it'll be better to just use someone else in those cases.

tl;dr S2 decently graded for the rarity, probably B-tier. S1 ungraded, but an option.
